### Why become a member?

Fleinkontroll is run by Tryggere Ruspolitikk (Safer Drug Policy). We work for a safer drug policy, based on knowledge instead of punishment.

As a member, you help support:

- **Rusopplysningen** - free, level-headed information about drugs, without moralizing.
- **The analysis service** - free, anonymous drug checking.
- **Safe Space** - help and safety at festivals.
- **Eikaklinikken** - health care for people with addiction.
- **Our work for better drug policy.**

As a member, you can:

- Take part in your local chapter's meetings and vote.
- Elect delegates to the national congress.
- Receive our newsletter on what's happening, locally and nationally.

The number of members matters: the more of us there are, the more operating support we get from the Norwegian Directorate of Health. Your membership helps keep services like Fleinkontroll running.
