### How does the service work?

You upload photos of your mushrooms, and one of our mushroom experts reviews them and gives you feedback based on visible characteristics - completely free, and without judgment.

### How long does it take to get a response?

We usually respond within 1-2 business days. During peak season, like early autumn, it can take a bit longer - we're working through requests as fast as we can.

### Do I need to register?

No. All submissions are anonymous. Keep your reference code so you can check back on your submission later.

### What happens after I submit?

You'll receive a reference code. Use it to retrieve your response once the assessment is ready.

### Is the assessment 100% certain?

No. The assessment is based on photos and visible features, and should never be your sole basis for eating a mushroom. If you're unsure - leave it be.
